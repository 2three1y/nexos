    About

    NexOS Web is a web twin of NexOS, matching NexOS v0.4. The real NexOS kernel is written in Rust for 64-bit x86 PCs. It runs in QEMU or on real hardware, and plays its sounds on the PC speaker.

    This page is not an emulator, and it does not run the real kernel. It mirrors the same nexos> shell, App Store and apps in plain HTML and JavaScript, so you can try them in any browser with a screen reader.

    Every button and key here has its own short, classic-style sound: numbered buttons rise in pitch, each app has its own little tune, quitting is a low thunk, and typing clicks softly. Type sound list to hear them described, or sound keys off to silence just the typing clicks. All of it follows the volume slider and Mute.

    The real kernel's source code: github.com/2three1y/nexos.

    Everything stays on your device. There are no network requests, no tracking, and it keeps working offline. Your files, notes and installed apps are saved in this browser only.
